How a user reaches the workspace

01VisitorExplores the public site, the demonstration and the published reports.
02Create accountRegisters with a work email, or is invited by an organisation administrator.
03Choose accessFree registered account, or a professional, institutional, government, enterprise or Academy licence.
04VerifyEmail verification, and institutional verification where the licence requires it.
05OrganisationJoins an existing organisation, or one is created with an administrator.
06SubscriptionLicence activated; model, agent, country and report permissions applied.
07WorkspaceSigned in, with the sidebar, Ask EDIS, scenarios, projects, maps and reports.
