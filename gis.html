Product

Not only what is needed — where

A geographic information system lets EDIS answer questions about location: which communities lack access to a service, where a facility should be sited, who a road actually reaches, and how a scenario changes the map.

Maps workspace

Layers, queries and scenario overlays

The maps workspace combines the spatial layers a planning ministry already holds with the results of a model run, so a scenario can be read as a map rather than a table.

Available layers

  • Population and settlements
  • Administrative boundaries
  • Roads and the transport network
  • Health facilities
  • Schools and education facilities
  • Energy infrastructure and the grid
  • Water points and schemes
  • Agricultural land and cropping
  • Mineral occurrences and licences
  • Industrial areas and parks
  • Broadband and backbone coverage
  • Climate hazard and exposure
  • Projects and the investment pipeline
  • Scenario result overlays

Spatial analysis

What the spatial layer computes

Access

Catchments and coverage

Which population a facility actually serves, and which settlements fall outside every catchment. The basis for a defensible statement about who is unserved.

Access

Travel time surfaces

Time to the nearest functioning facility over the real network, not straight-line distance. Recomputed when a road investment changes the network.

Planning

Siting options

Candidate locations ranked by population served, cost, network access and overlap with existing provision, passed to the appraisal system.

Planning

Gap analysis

Where demand and provision diverge most, by district and by service, with the standard the gap is measured against stated explicitly.

Investment

Investment mapping

Where the pipeline is concentrated, where it is absent, and how a proposed portfolio changes the regional distribution of provision.

Scenario

Scenario overlays

Model results mapped back onto geography: poverty change by region, access change by district, output change by cropping zone.

Worked example

Where should the next health centres go?

The Health Agent asks the Health model for the facility requirement, UCSN for what each facility costs to build and to run, GIS for the candidate locations and the population each would serve, and PIAS to rank them within the budget available.
