Request a demonstration

Tell us the country, the sector and the question. A demonstration is arranged against a real policy question your institution is facing, not a generic tour of the interface.

We reply from info@edisgov.org.
What happens next

Three steps

01Scoping call Thirty minutes to establish the question, the country, the data available and who needs to be in the room.
02Demonstration EDIS is run against your question, with the model chain, the provenance and the limitations shown as they would be in production.
03Licence discussion Seats, countries, run allowance, data integration, support and training, agreed as a contract rather than a published price.
