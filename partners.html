Training partnership

The partner teaches; AICTEM keeps the standard

A training partner delivers EDIS Academy courses to its own participants, using its own faculty. Platform access, training datasets, model versions, assessment and certification stay under AICTEM management, so a certificate means the same thing wherever it was earned.

What the partner receives

  • Course materials, exercises and training datasets
  • Trainer certification for the partner’s faculty
  • Laboratory environments provisioned per cohort
  • Participant registration and progress tracking
  • Joint certification under the Academy register
  • A defined route from Academy access to an institutional licence
Expectations

What the partnership requires

  • Faculty who complete trainer certification before delivering a course
  • Delivery to the published course specification and assessment standard
  • Participant records maintained in the Academy register
  • Training datasets used only inside the laboratory environment
  • No onward licensing of platform access to third parties

Interested?

Research partnership

Work that returns to the platform

Research partnerships are structured so that calibration, validation and country adaptation work becomes part of the maintained model estate rather than ending with the project.

Contribution

Calibration and data

New social accounting matrices, elasticity sets, unit-cost studies and country datasets, registered with provenance and a quality score.

Contribution

Validation

Back-testing, sensitivity work and independent replication that moves a model from partial to validated status for a country.

Contribution

Extension

New sector modules or methods, specified against the platform’s interfaces so they can be called by agents like any other model service.
