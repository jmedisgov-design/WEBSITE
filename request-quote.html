Request a quote, proposal or demonstration

Government and institutional buyers rarely purchase with a card. Tell us what you need and in what form — a quote, a proforma invoice, a formal proposal, a demonstration or a sandbox on sample data — and AICTEM will respond with a document your procurement process can use.

What happens next

Four steps

01Scoping A short call to establish the institution, the country, the policy questions and who needs to be in the room. No commitment.
02Demonstration EDIS is run against one of your own questions, with the model chain, the provenance and the limitations shown as they would be in production.
03Proposal A written commercial proposal: subscription, implementation, training and support, with development access pricing applied where it is available.
04Contract and implementation Country configuration, data setup, calibration, user provisioning, training and testing, against an agreed plan.

Procurement without a card

Institutional contracts support quotes, proforma invoices, formal proposals, purchase orders, framework agreements and development-partner procurement. Only the individual plans use online payment.
