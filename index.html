How EDIS works

A question becomes a routed workflow

EDIS never sends a policy question straight to a language model and returns the reply. The question is classified, the country and sector identified, the agents selected, the models determined, the data and permissions checked, and only then is a workflow executed.

01QuestionA policy question in ordinary language, with country, region, horizon and currency.
02OrchestratorIntent classification, country and sector identification, scenario detection.
03Agent selectionThe registered agents that accept this class of question are assembled.
04Data and modelsRequired model services are resolved; data availability and permissions are checked.
05SimulationThe workflow is queued and executed by model workers, with live status.
06ValidationConvergence, plausibility and consistency checks before anything is shown.
07Policy intelligenceResults assembled into summary, charts, maps, budget and distributional views.
08ReportA downloadable brief or technical report with full provenance and a run ID.

Cross-model workflow

You can see what EDIS is doing

A single question can run eight models in sequence. EDIS shows the chain as it executes, marks each model complete, running, waiting or failed, and stops rather than guessing when a model does not converge or a dataset is missing.

Design principle

EDIS is not a chatbot with a policy opinion

The AI layer and the analytical layer are separated by design and the separation is enforced in the architecture, not merely described in the marketing.

The AI layer

  • Understands the question
  • Routes it to registered agents
  • Orchestrates the model workflow
  • Explains what the results mean
  • Writes the report around them

The EDIS analytical layer

  • Calculates and forecasts
  • Simulates scenarios
  • Optimizes allocations
  • Maps and analyses space
  • Validates every result
