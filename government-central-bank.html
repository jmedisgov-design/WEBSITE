Questions it answers

The questions a central bank brings

Outlook

What is the growth outlook under lower commodity prices?

FINEX runs the baseline and the commodity scenario side by side, with the external assumptions explicit.

Inflation

How does a depreciation feed into inflation?

The exchange-rate pass-through is modelled through the external sector into prices, with the elasticity stated and sourced.

Policy

What happens if we hold the rate for another two quarters?

A policy path is simulated against the baseline and compared, with the difference attributed rather than asserted.

Consistency

Is our forecast consistent with the fiscal frame?

The bank and the ministry can run on the same calibration, so a difference between two forecasts is a difference in assumptions, not in data.

Economy-wide

What does this do beyond the aggregates?

CDCGE closes the economy and reports sectoral output, factor incomes and prices, not just the headline.

Research

Can we publish from it?

Every run carries its model, version, data, scenario and run ID, so a published result can be reproduced.
