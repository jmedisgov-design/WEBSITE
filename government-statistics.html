Statistics Office

The data every model downstream depends on

A statistics office is the upstream of the whole estate. If the national accounts, the SAM and the household survey are not governed, versioned and published properly, every model in every ministry inherits the problem silently. EDIS makes that dependency explicit and manageable.

Questions it answers

The questions a statistics office brings

Governance

Who is using which vintage of our data?

Every model run records the dataset and version it used, so a revision can be traced to everything it affects.

Quality

Where is our data too thin to support the model?

Quality control is explicit, and EDIS states where the data cannot support a model rather than substituting a proxy.

Publication

How do we stop serving spreadsheets by email?

Governed data APIs give ministries and researchers one publication point, within permissions.

Calibration

Can our SAM support the CGE the ministry wants to run?

The calibration tools show what the SAM supports and what it does not, before a model is built on it.

Surveys

Are the poverty numbers computed correctly?

Household analysis handles weights, strata and clusters with design-based standard errors, and states the accounting unit and the denominator.

Revision

What happens when we revise the accounts?

The revision propagates as a new vintage, and every downstream result says which vintage it used.
