What each institution actually runs

Workflows, not feature lists. Each of these is a chain of models that starts with a question a real institution has to answer and ends with a decision it has to defend.

Institution 01

Ministry of Finance

The envelope, the ceilings, the appraisal and what the budget buys. FINEX sets the macro-fiscal frame, MTEF allocates it, PIMAPS ranks the pipeline, the Budget Output Engine says what the allocation produces, and CDCGE closes the economy on the result.
