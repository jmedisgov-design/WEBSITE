Pricing

EDIS pricing for professionals, institutions and governments

Economic intelligence from individual analysis to national decision infrastructure. EDIS is economic development intelligence infrastructure — integrated policy modelling, public investment and budget intelligence, sector planning and economy-wide analysis. Institutional pricing reflects model configuration, national data integration, calibration, training and support, so it is priced by institution rather than by seat.

Implementation & configuration

Charged separately from the subscription

A subscription grants access. Implementation makes EDIS work for one country and one institution, and is a one-time engagement quoted from the scope. The figures are indicative minimums, not fixed prices.

    Development access pricing

    Pricing that reflects fiscal capacity

    The institutions that most need economic decision infrastructure are often those least able to pay a high-income price for it. Development access pricing adjusts institutional subscriptions against the country’s income classification. It is applied at contract.

    Donor-supported deployment

    A deployment can be financed by a development partner, by the government, or shared between a donor, the government and AICTEM. If you are a development partner considering funding EDIS for a government, we scope it with you and the beneficiary together.

    Licence architecture

    Access is a licence, not a page

    What a user may ask, which models they may run, which countries they may see, how many runs they have and what they may export are all set by the subscription. The plans above are the published packages; underneath, licences are configured records, so a package can be changed without touching the platform.

    Capability matrix

    What each access level includes

    Capability PublicRegisteredProfessional InstitutionalGovernmentEnterprise

    Academy access is granted for the duration of a course and is not shown as a column; API access is added to any level from Professional upward. Institutional, government and enterprise terms — seats, countries, run allowances, data integration and support — are set in the contract.

    Configurable licensing

    Packages are data, not code

    Administrators alter what a package contains without a software release. The subscription layer holds plans, features, subscriptions, organisations, users, roles, permissions, usage limits, model permissions, agent permissions, API limits, permitted countries, permitted reports, storage limits, expiry and billing status.

    Contracts supported include monthly and annual subscriptions, institutional contracts, country licences, enterprise agreements, training licences and trial accounts.

    No page on this site contains a price. Every figure published above is rendered from one canonical record, and a contracted figure comes from the licence record for the organisation concerned.
